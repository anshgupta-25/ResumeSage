Senior Backend Engineer — Platform Team

We are looking for a senior backend engineer to help scale our payments
platform, which currently handles about 2 million transactions a day.

What you will do:
- Design and build distributed backend services in Python
- Own service reliability, including participating in an on-call rotation
- Lead the migration of legacy monolith endpoints to microservices
- Mentor mid-level engineers and lead design reviews

Required:
- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience
- Deep Python expertise, ideally with FastAPI or similar async frameworks
- Strong SQL and data modelling skills, PostgreSQL preferred
- Production experience with Kubernetes
- Experience with a message broker such as Kafka or RabbitMQ
- Track record of owning services in production at scale

Nice to have:
- Experience in fintech or payments
- Terraform / infrastructure as code
- Go or Rust for performance-critical paths
- Observability tooling: Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry
